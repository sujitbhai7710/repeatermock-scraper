Minimize single-use refresh-token rotations (long-term chain stability)

The refresh token is single-use and rotates on every /auth/refresh.
Every avoidable rotation was a chance to lose the chain forever.

- New access_token_seconds_left() decodes the JWT exp of the access
  token, so refresh decisions are driven by real expiry, not habit.
- Startup: no longer force-refreshes unconditionally after
  refresh_cookies_if_needed just rotated — that was 2 rotations per
  run where 1 sufficed. Now only force-refreshes when the access
  token has <3 min left.
- Recovery (wait_for_live_account): skips the verification
  force-refresh when the auth refresh already rotated the token —
  a successful rotation IS the proof the chain works.
- Proactive refresh stays at 10 min (inside the 15-min window).

Net effect: ~1 rotation per scheduled run instead of 3+, chain
sustains indefinitely once started with one valid cookie set.
